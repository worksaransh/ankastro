# AnkJyotish AI — Multi-Role Admin & RBAC Guide

## 1. Role Matrix
The platform enforces server-side role-based access control across 11 roles:

1. **Super Admin**: Complete master authority over all system configurations, database tables, billing, and user roles.
2. **General Admin**: Day-to-day operations, user management, and report generation audits.
3. **Astrology Admin**: Management of Vedic planetary formulas, ayanamsa systems (Lahiri, KP, Western), and Yogas.
4. **Numerology Admin**: Management of NIKB tables, compound number interpretations, 81-matrix records, and Loshu rules.
5. **AI Admin**: Management of AI prompt templates, model selection (Groq, Gemini), token quotas, and response audit logs.
6. **Content & CMS Admin**: Management of blog articles, landing pages, testimonials, FAQs, and SEO meta tags.
7. **E-Commerce Admin**: Management of Mulank T-Shirts, variant matrices, apparel inventory, and fulfillment tracking.
8. **Affiliate Admin**: Management of merchant links, click telemetry, conversion attribution, and commissions.
9. **Marketing Admin**: Management of UTM campaigns, social acquisition funnels, and promotional coupons.
10. **Support Admin**: Management of customer tickets, report regeneration requests, and refund processing.
11. **Analytics Admin**: Real-time event analytics, retention cohorts, and conversion funnel dashboards.
