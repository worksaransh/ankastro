# AnkJyotish Atelier — D2C E-Commerce & T-Shirt Engine

## 1. Concept & Vision
AnkJyotish Atelier bridges high-fashion streetwear with cosmic frequencies.
Every t-shirt is designed around the 9 Mulank numbers and 12 Zodiac archetypes.

## 2. Product Architecture
- **Material Specs**: 240 GSM heavy combed luxury cotton, pre-shrunk, bio-washed.
- **Graphic Technique**: 24K gold foil metallic glyphs and sacred geometry prints.
- **Personalization**: Laser-engraved inner collar with user's name and birth vibration seal.

## 3. Order & Checkout Pipeline
```text
User Birth Profile / Mulank (e.g. 1)
   ↓
Automated Recommendation
   ↓
Product Configurator (Size, Color, Name Embossing)
   ↓
Cart Context & Multi-Item Bag
   ↓
Encrypted Checkout (Address + Coupon Code)
   ↓
Cashfree / Razorpay Gateway Payment
   ↓
Order Item Dispatch via Delhivery / BlueDart
```
